**ProSwisher Privacy Policy**
Effective: [DATE] · Version: privacy-b1

This policy explains how **Op3x LLC** ("we," "us"), an Illinois limited liability company, collects and uses personal information through ProSwisher (proswisher.com).

**1. What we collect**
- **Visitors (no account needed).** Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs: IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. When you confirm you're 21+, your browser remembers it for the session in session storage on your device. It isn't sent to us. We set no advertising cookies.
- **Members.**
  - Your email address and a hashed password, or a sign-in link.
  - Subscription status and dates.
  - A **consent record** made when you subscribe: date and time, IP address, the version of the checkout text you agreed to, the price shown, and your confirmation that you're 21 or older.
- **Payments.** Stripe, Inc. collects your card details and billing address (country and ZIP) directly. We never see or store your full card number. We get back limited information: the last 4 digits, card brand, billing country and ZIP, and payment status.
- **Support.** What you send us when you email support@proswisher.com.

**2. How we use it**
- **To provide the membership.** Sign-in, access to every game, renewals, receipts and the required account emails. Those emails are your confirmation, the annual reminder, notices of changes, and cancellation confirmations.
- **To check where subscriptions are available.** At checkout we use your IP address to estimate your country and state, and we check your billing country and ZIP code, so we can confirm subscriptions are available where you are. We use this only for that check and for our consent and fraud records. Subscriptions aren't available in Washington, Hawaii, Utah or Japan.
- **To keep a record of your consent** to the 21+ confirmation and the automatic renewal terms, and to handle refunds, disputes and chargebacks.
- **To secure the site,** prevent fraud and enforce our Terms. That includes detecting scraping or reselling of member predictions.
- **To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.**

We don't use your information to build advertising profiles. We don't sell it or share it for targeted advertising. We don't use AI to process your personal information.

**3. Service providers**
We share personal information only with providers that help us run ProSwisher, under terms that limit how they use it:
- **Stripe, Inc.**: payment processing, subscription billing, tax calculation, fraud prevention and disputes. Stripe also uses some payment data as its own controller under its own privacy policy.
- **Railway Corp.**: hosting and database.
- **[Email provider, e.g. Resend / Google Workspace: name the one actually used]**: account email delivery and our support inbox.
- **[IP geolocation provider, only if a third-party service is used; omit if it's a local database]**: location check at checkout.

Data is processed in the United States. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or users, or as part of a sale or transfer of the business.

**4. Cookies and similar technologies**
We use only what the site needs to work: session storage for the age confirmation and, for members, a sign-in cookie. We use **no analytics or advertising cookies or pixels**. If that ever changes, we'll update this policy first and ask for consent where the law requires it.

**5. How long we keep it**
We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, and delete it **as soon as practicable** after that. The exception is information the law requires us to keep, such as payment, tax and consent records needed for refunds, chargebacks or legal claims; we keep that only for the period the law requires or the dispute lasts. Server logs are deleted as soon as practicable. If you cancel, your account information is deleted as soon as practicable after your paid period ends, except for those required records.

**6. Your privacy rights**
Depending on where you live, including California (CCPA/CPRA) and other U.S. states with privacy laws, you may have the right to:
- know what personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
- correct it or delete it;
- opt out of "sale," "sharing" for targeted advertising, or profiling. **We don't do any of these.**

We won't treat you differently for using these rights. To make a request, email support@proswisher.com from the email on your account. We'll confirm your identity and respond within 45 days. An authorized agent may make a request for you with your signed permission. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer, and we'll respond within the time your state's law requires.

California notice at collection: we collect the categories listed in §1 for the purposes in §2 and keep them as described in §5. We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't use or disclose sensitive personal information except to provide the service. Your card details are handled by Stripe.

**7. Security**
We use reasonable safeguards: HTTPS, restricted access, a dedicated restricted payment key, and no card numbers on our servers. No system is perfectly secure.

**8. Adults only**
ProSwisher is only for adults **21 and older**. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. If we learn we have, we delete it and close the account.

**9. Changes**
If we change this policy, we'll post the new version with its effective date. If the change is material, we'll email members before it takes effect.
