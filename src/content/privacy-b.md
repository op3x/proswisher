**ProSwisher Privacy Policy**
Effective: [DATE] · Version: privacy-b2

This policy explains how **Op3x LLC** ("we," "us"), an Illinois limited liability company, collects and uses personal information through ProSwisher (proswisher.com).

**1. What we collect**
- **Visitors (no account needed).**
  - *Server logs.* Our hosting provider, Railway Corp., automatically logs each request: IP address, browser type (user agent), the page requested and the time.
  - *Visit counting.* Our own server counts visits without cookies. To recognize repeat visits on the same day, it combines a shortened form of your IP address and your user agent with a random value, and turns them into a one-way code (a hash). The random value is held only in memory, is replaced every day and is never saved. We keep only totals: daily unique visitors, featured-game viewers, membership-button clicks, and visits and clicks by marketing campaign. We don't count visits from the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland. We don't count visits from browsers that send a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal.
  - *Age confirmation.* When you confirm you're 21+, your browser remembers it for the session in session storage on your device. It isn't sent to us.
- **Members.**
  - *From Stripe:* your name, email address, billing address, the last 4 digits and brand of your card, and your subscription status and dates. Stripe, Inc. collects your card details directly, and we never see or store your full card number.
  - *Membership reference:* after checkout, your browser stores your Stripe checkout reference in local storage on your device, so the site can recognize your membership. It isn't a cookie, and clearing your browser storage removes it.
  - *Consent record,* made when you subscribe: the date and time, **your IP address**, the version of the checkout text you agreed to, the price shown, your confirmation that you're 21 or older, the billing country and state you entered, and the country and state estimated from your IP address. It's stored with your subscription at Stripe and in our records.
- **Support.** What you send us when you email support@proswisher.com.

**2. How we use it**
- **To run and improve the site.** We use server logs to operate, debug and secure the site, and aggregate visit totals to understand how many people use it and which of our marketing links work. We don't use them to profile you or target ads, and we don't try to re-identify anyone from the totals.
- **To provide the membership.** Sign-in, access to every game, renewals, receipts and the required account emails. Those emails are your confirmation, the annual reminder, notices of changes, and cancellation confirmations.
- **To check where subscriptions are available.** When you open the subscription page or start checkout, our server estimates your country and state from your IP address using a location database stored on our own server; your IP address isn't sent to a location service. We also check the billing country and state or ZIP you give. We keep only the country and state codes (plus the IP address in the consent record described above). Subscriptions aren't available in Washington, Hawaii, Utah or Japan.
- **To keep a record of your consent** to the 21+ confirmation and the automatic renewal terms, and to handle refunds, disputes and chargebacks.
- **To secure the site,** prevent fraud and enforce our Terms. That includes detecting scraping or reselling of member predictions.
- **To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.**

We don't use your information to build advertising profiles. We don't sell it or share it for targeted advertising. We don't use AI to process your personal information.

**3. Service providers**
We share personal information only with providers that help us run ProSwisher, under terms that limit how they use it:
- **Stripe, Inc.**: payment processing, subscription billing, tax calculation, fraud prevention and disputes. Stripe also uses some payment data as its own controller under its own privacy policy.
- **Railway Corp.**: hosting and server logs.
- **[Email provider, e.g. Resend / Google Workspace: name the one actually used]**: account email delivery and our support inbox.

Data is processed in the United States. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or users, or as part of a sale or transfer of the business.

**4. Cookies and similar technologies**
We don't use cookies. We use only what the site needs to work: session storage for the age confirmation and, for members, local storage for your checkout reference. Our visit counting uses no cookies or device storage, and we use **no analytics or advertising cookies, pixels or third-party trackers**. If that ever changes, we'll update this policy first and ask for consent where the law requires it.

**5. How long we keep it**
- **Visit-counting codes:** discarded every day, when the daily random value is replaced. **Visit totals:** up to 25 months; totals by individual marketing link are combined into monthly campaign totals after 30 days.
- **Server logs:** kept by Railway for up to 90 days, then deleted.
- **Consent records** (including the IP address): for as long as your subscription is active and for 4 years after it ends, so we can answer refund, chargeback and legal claims. After that we delete the IP address.
- **Payment, tax and accounting records:** for the period the law requires.
- **Everything else:** deleted as soon as practicable once we no longer need it for the purposes above. If you cancel, we delete your membership information as soon as practicable after your paid period ends, except the records listed above.

**6. Your privacy rights**
Depending on where you live, including California (CCPA/CPRA) and other U.S. states with privacy laws, you may have the right to:
- know what personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
- correct it or delete it;
- opt out of "sale," "sharing" for targeted advertising, or profiling. **We don't do any of these.**

Visit totals are aggregate and de-identified. We maintain them in that form and don't attempt to re-identify them, so they aren't tied to any individual request.

We won't treat you differently for using these rights. To make a request, email support@proswisher.com from the email on your account. We'll confirm your identity and respond within 45 days. An authorized agent may make a request for you with your signed permission. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer, and we'll respond within the time your state's law requires.

California notice at collection: we collect the categories listed in §1 for the purposes in §2 and keep them as described in §5. We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't use or disclose sensitive personal information except to provide the service. Your card details are handled by Stripe.

**7. Security**
We use reasonable safeguards: HTTPS, restricted access, a dedicated restricted payment key, and no card numbers on our servers. No system is perfectly secure.

**8. Adults only**
ProSwisher is only for adults **21 and older**. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. If we learn we have, we delete it and close the account.

**9. Changes**
If we change this policy, we'll post the new version with its effective date. If the change is material, we'll email members before it takes effect.
